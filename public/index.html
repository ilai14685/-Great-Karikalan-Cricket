ℹ️ About this Game
Great Karikalan Cricket is a fast hand-cricket game you play right in the browser — no app install needed. Pick a number each ball, outsmart your opponent or the AI, and chase the target before time runs out.
🏏 How to Play
  • 1Win the toss (or call heads/tails) and choose to bat or bowl first.
  • 2Each ball, both the batter and bowler secretly pick a number from 1–6.
  • 3If both numbers match, the batter is OUT. If they differ, the batter scores that many runs.
  • 4After 12 balls or a wicket, the innings ends and the target is set for the chase.
  • 5If scores are tied after both innings, a 3-ball Super Over decides the winner — tied again? Sudden-death Super 1!
